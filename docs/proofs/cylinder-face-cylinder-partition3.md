# Cylinder face partition by another cylinder

`partitionCylinderFaceByCylinder3` constructs an owned partition of a finite cylinder band/angular patch by a second circular cylinder. It returns embedded monotone strip domains, exact point location, oriented native/SSI boundary uses, and a shared-edge incidence model. It retains artificial generator subdivisions; cells are not maximal connected faces. It does not claim general solid Boolean validity or SolidCore API parity.

## Complete angular decomposition

For nonparallel supports, use the complete supporting SSI graph and restrict it to the source face's rational axial interval. Project all original SSI vertices and all height-clipping vertices to the source reference circle. These include discriminant-critical fibers, source representation seams, height-bound crossings and isolated contacts. Together with the source angular endpoints and four cardinal chart points, they partition the angular domain into proper injective circle arcs. Every retained SSI branch either covers a complete open angular cell or misses it. Evaluate each covering branch at the cell endpoints and split it into a boundary arc for that cell.

The ordered axial bounds are the source lower bound, zero to two retained SSI roots, and the source upper bound. Roots cannot change order inside a cell: a meeting is a discriminant event, and a crossing of a constant bound is a height-clipping event. Consecutive distinct bounds define a nonempty open strip. Endpoint bounds are checked to remain noncrossing. Isolating interval refinement chooses a rational height strictly between the two algebraic bounds at an interior angular point. This avoids combining unrelated algebraic root fields just to obtain a witness. The cutter polynomial has no zero in the open strip, so its sign is constant and labels the cell negative, positive, or (for coincident supports only) zero.

Parallel supports have no height-dependent roots. Their common generators supply the complete angular events; tangency retains same-sign adjacent cells. Coincident supports give zero-labelled cells. Disjoint supports give their constant sign. Thus no unsupported supporting intersection family is silently treated as empty.

## Embedded faces and correspondence

A proper angular cell maps injectively onto its source reference-circle arc. The axial coordinate supplies an injective cylinder parameterization `p=q+d*h`. Continuous, strictly ordered interior bounds therefore define an embedded open disk; the closed bounds may meet at one or both endpoints, collapsing generator sides. Each domain retains its angular arc and lower/upper constant or owned-SSI bounds. `locate` checks source cylinder membership, angular position and exact axial inequalities. For SSI bounds, queries compare axial position to the selected root using the sign of the cutter quadratic and the sign of its derivative (position relative to the quadratic vertex). A positive quadratic is positive outside its roots and negative between them; the derivative distinguishes the two outside intervals and selects the zero branch. This avoids reconstructing nested radicals per query. The source domain is reconstructed; external callbacks are not trusted.

For outward source sense, the lower boundary runs CCW about the source axis, the end generator upward, the upper boundary backward and the start generator downward. Inward sense reverses the entire loop. Zero-length generators are omitted. Isolated SSI contacts node the artificial generator boundaries instead of creating zero-area faces. Every native or SSI boundary is bound to the same domain endpoints, and sewing preserves ordered source uses. Neighboring strips have opposite shared coedge directions. Their interiors are disjoint, and their closed union covers the original source face.

This constructive domain argument adds information beyond the generic incidence builder; the incidence builder alone still does not certify arbitrary faces. Artificial boundary edges on the original face remain open, as expected for a surface patch.

## Validation and limits

Tests check domain coverage with independent rational circle/height samples, lack of duplicate interior ownership, exact sign labels, source-boundary ownership of open edges, loop continuity, whole-edge support and opposite shared uses. Fixtures include crossing ellipses, genuine quartic SSI with height cuts, algebraic angular endpoints, inward sense, parallel crossings and tangency, coincident/disjoint supports, isolated skew tangency, partial real angular fibers, a dependent-normal SSI node, and nonorthogonal axes with the independent quadratic `(z-x)²+2*y²-2`.

The result is a partition of one band/angular face by one cylinder support. It is not a split by a finite cutter face or solid: cutter caps and other faces must also contribute constraints. Arbitrary subsequent cutters, maximal face consolidation, general NURBS surfaces, persistent mixed Body adoption, oriented planar pcurve atlases, global shell embedding and material Boolean assembly remain required work. The construction and executable tests are not new machine proofs or whole-program TypeScript verification.
